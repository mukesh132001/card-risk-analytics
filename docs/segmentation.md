# Customer segmentation

Generated by `python -m card_analytics.segment`. K-means on 12 standardised behaviour features per account
(money in/out per month, transactions per month, balance, overdrafts, cash/card/transfer/loan mix, pension and cash income, card ownership),
built from the PII-masked reporting layer. Segment 1 has the highest money in per month.

## Choosing k

Silhouette score (higher = tighter, better-separated groups) for k = 2..8. Picked **k = 4**, the best score between 3 and 6 segments (a usable number for a business).

## What sets each segment apart

- **Segment 1:** higher has a card (+2.0 sd); higher card spend, share of money out (+1.2 sd); higher average balance (log) (+1.0 sd).
- **Segment 2:** higher money out per month (log) (+0.5 sd); higher money in per month (log) (+0.5 sd); lower has a card (-0.5 sd).
- **Segment 3:** higher cash withdrawals, share of money out (+1.4 sd); lower transfers to other banks, share of money out (-1.3 sd); lower transactions per month (-1.1 sd).
- **Segment 4:** lower cash deposits, share of money in (-1.3 sd); higher pension, share of money in (+1.2 sd); lower money in per month (log) (-1.1 sd).

`sd` = standard deviations from the all-account average on that feature. Segment labels are in `rpt_account_segments` for dashboards.
